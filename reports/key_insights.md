# Key Insights

- **Total revenue:** 7,114,542 EGP across 3,269 orders (1510 unique customers, 2024-01-01 to 2025-12-30)
- **Average order value:** 2,176 EGP
- **Best month:** December 2025; **slowest month:** September 2024
- **Last vs first month growth:** +68.1%
- **Top category:** Electronics (47.2% of revenue)
- **Top region:** Cairo (39.4% of revenue)
- **Most used payment method:** Credit Card (35.5% of revenue)
- **Repeat customer rate:** 65.9% of customers ordered more than once

## Business takeaways

1. **Cairo dominates revenue.** Marketing budget and stock should follow that demand, with targeted offers to grow the weaker regions.
2. **Electronics is the revenue engine.** Protecting its availability and pricing matters more than discounting weaker categories.
3. **Clear seasonality.** Revenue peaks around spring and year-end; staffing and campaigns should be planned a month ahead of each peak.
4. **Credit Card leads payments.** Keeping that method friction-free (and nudging the long tail toward it) reduces checkout drop-off.
5. **Repeat rate of 66%.** A loyalty or re-engagement push on one-time buyers is the cheapest revenue upside in the data.
